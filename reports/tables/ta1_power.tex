\caption[What each design could have detected]{\textbf{The smallest cost difference each design could have detected, by environment and cell.} Every headline verdict in this chapter is a null, and a null is informative only if the instrument could have seen the effect had it been present. \textbf{MDE} is the effect size the registered across-seed test would detect with 80\% probability at $\alpha=0.05$, computed from the exact non-central $t$ distribution rather than a normal approximation, which at five seeds overstates power substantially. \textbf{The interval is the claim, not the point estimate}: with five seeds the across-seed spread is itself estimated on four degrees of freedom, and the interval shown propagates that uncertainty. $\sigma_{\text{ep}}$ is per-episode cost dispersion, $\sigma_{\text{seed}}$ the spread across independently trained seeds, and $\rho$ the correlation between seeds' per-episode difference series induced by common random numbers. Shared noise shifts every seed mean together and so contributes nothing to their spread, which is why the test is powerful against differences between policies and powerless against a shift common to all of them. \textsuperscript{\dag}\,\textbf{Not quotable}: fewer than three seeds survived the behaviour audit. Unfinished orders are forced to complete at the deadline, and the audit removes agents that rely on that forced purchase \emph{before} any cost is compared; below three survivors the spread rests on one degree of freedom. The lower panel reports the one thing that licenses applying a sensitivity measured on one block to a verdict issued on another: dispersion was measured on two or three blocks per environment and agrees to within 7.3\% everywhere. Recorded order books are the least sensitive of the three environments, and the cause is stated per cell rather than generalised. The weakest remaining cell is the value-based learner at 193\,BTC on the sealed split, with an MDE of 0.623\,bps and a 0.075 probability of detecting a difference at the 0.05\,bps materiality threshold; its across-seed spread is 85\% policy-to-policy variation, so that cell is a convergence problem rather than a sampling one.
